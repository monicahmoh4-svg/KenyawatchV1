HOW IT WORKS

From Raw Contracts to Citizen Power

A transparent, four-step pipeline turns public procurement data into actionable intelligence.

📥
01

1. Ingest

Pull machine-readable records from the Open Contracting Data Standard (OCDS) feed, plus documented public-interest cases.

🧠
02

2. Score

A transparent rules engine scores every contract for risk — bid type, value, supplier timing, and vague scope.

🛰️
03

3. Verify

Cross-check claimed project completion against live satellite imagery to expose "ghost" projects.

🔔
04

4. Act

Citizens report anonymously, AI triages credibility, and cases route to EACC, PPRA, or the DPP.

Data Sources & Methodology

📄 DOCUMENTED

Documented Cases

Real, source-cited procurement cases gathered from public records, investigative journalism, and official audit reports. These carry the highest confidence.

🔄 LIVE SYNC

Live OCDS Sync

Machine-readable records pulled directly from the Open Contracting Data Standard (OCDS) feed where available. Kenya's PPIP is progressively adopting this standard.

📊 REFERENCE

Reference Baseline

Representative sample contracts generated to ensure every county and sector has a baseline for UI testing and risk-scoring demonstration. Clearly labeled as such.

🤖 MANUAL SCAN

Manual AI Scans

Contracts manually submitted by users or investigators through the "Scan New Contract" feature, processed through our risk engine in real-time.
